What the prototype proves

The Cut flow end to end: resolve → transcribe → propose → review → rebuild → verify. Unchecking a cut updates the waveform, the reclaimed figure and the resulting duration together, because that linkage is the thing an editor has to trust before they press Apply.

Why local matters

Transcription runs on the workstation GPU via faster-whisper. Client footage never leaves the machine and an hour of rushes costs nothing to process — the claim competitors routing through a cloud gateway cannot make.

Rebuild, not razor

Premiere 2026 silently no-ops the undocumented QE razor, so kept spans are re-laid with overwriteClip and the result is checked against the expected duration. Reclaimed time stays as a gap until you explicitly close it.
